# Aziz HRM — User guide

The interface is available in English and Arabic (top bar → **العربية / English**), in light or dark mode, on desktop, tablet and phone. Press **Ctrl+K** to search for an employee from anywhere.

## Everyone (self-service)

- **Home** — check in and out (on a phone your location may be checked), see your leave balances, recent requests, announcements and latest payslip.
- **Leave → Request leave** — choose the type and dates. The form shows how many days will be counted and what will be left *before* you submit. Attach a document when the leave type requires one. You can cancel a pending request, or an approved one that hasn't started yet.
- **Attendance** — your records and monthly totals. Use **Request correction** if a punch is missing or wrong, and **Request overtime** to have extra hours approved.
- **My payslips** — payslips appear once payroll is approved. Open one to see every line, or download the PDF.
- **My profile** (avatar menu) — view your record and update your phone, personal email, address and emergency contact.
- **Approvals → My requests** — follow each request step by step, with every decision and comment.
- **Account settings** — change your password, turn on two-step verification, sign out other devices, and choose which notifications you get in the app or by email.

## Managers

- **Approvals → Waiting for me** — decide individually (a comment is required to reject) or select several and approve them together. **Delegations** lets a colleague approve for you while you're away.
- **Employees, Attendance, Leave, Dashboard and Reports** show only your team, the departments you manage, or your branch, depending on your role.
- **Leave calendar** shows who is away, with pending requests faded.

## HR

- **Employees → Add employee** — the employee number is generated automatically. The system warns about duplicates (same email, national ID, or name and date of birth). Tick *create a user account* to give the person self-service access. A temporary password is shown once.
- **Import** — download the template, fill it in, click **Validate** to see every problem by row, then **Import**. Nothing is saved until all rows are valid.
- Changing department, branch, position, grade or manager is recorded in the employee's **History**. Add a reason and effective date in the edit form.
- **Documents** — upload contracts, IDs, passports and certificates with expiry dates. Reminders go to HR (and to the employee for non-confidential documents) 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before expiry. Mark sensitive files *confidential*.
- **Record separation** (employee page → ⋯) — resignation, termination or retirement. Access ends on the last working day, and direct reports move to the person's manager. Use **Archive** only for records created by mistake.
- **Leave → Record leave for an employee** (optionally pre-approved) and **Adjust balance** (every adjustment is audited).
- **Attendance → Records** — add or correct a record. Late, early-leave, overtime and status are recalculated from the shift.
- **Settings** — company details and regional formats, attendance policy (grace, overtime approval, web/mobile check-in, geofence, device key), shifts (fixed, flexible, overnight), public holidays per branch, and leave policies (entitlement, accrual, carry-forward, notice, maximum length, attachments, gender, service requirement).
- **Organization** — departments, branches, positions with budgeted headcount, grades with salary ranges, cost centers, categories and employment types. **Workforce planning** compares filled positions with the budget.

## Payroll and finance

1. **Payroll → Pay components** — define allowances, deductions and employer contributions as fixed amounts, percentages of basic or gross, or progressive brackets, with caps and eligibility (category, nationality, employment type). Turn on *apply automatically* for statutory items. Nothing is preset by law, so configure what applies to you.
2. **Calculation rules** — daily-rate basis, overtime multipliers, unpaid-leave/absence/lateness deductions, proration and rounding.
3. **Salaries** — set or change an employee's salary on their **Salary** tab. Changes go through the *Salary change* workflow and take effect on the chosen date. Mid-month changes are prorated.
4. **Payroll runs → New payroll run** → **Calculate**. Check *Calculation issues* (employees without a salary, negative net pay), add one-off **Adjustments** if needed and recalculate. Review **Summary**: the totals must reconcile.
5. **Submit for approval**. The approver (e.g. a Finance Officer — never the person who submitted it) approves from **Approvals**. The period is then locked: attendance, leave and salary data for those dates can't be changed.
6. Download the **Bank file** (rows missing bank details are flagged), hold individual payments if necessary, then **Mark as paid**. Employees are notified that payslips are available.
7. Mistake found after approval? **Reopen** (reason required) → fix → recalculate → submit again. Paid payroll can't be reopened.

## Administrators

- **Users** — create accounts, assign roles, reset passwords, unlock accounts, and remove two-step verification for a lost device.
- **Roles & permissions** — edit what each role can do and its data scope, or create custom roles.
- **Approval workflows** — one or more workflows per request type, with steps, approver types, amount thresholds, escalation and conditions. Request types without a workflow are approved automatically.
- **Audit log** — filter by record, action and date. Open an entry to see before/after values.
- **System** — health, job history, and buttons to run maintenance jobs now.

## Reports

Choose a report, set filters, **Run**, then export to Excel, CSV or PDF. **Save configuration** keeps the filters. Add a schedule (daily, weekly, monthly) and recipients to have it emailed, with dates relative to the previous month if you like.
